Forecast · as of —

Reading the release calendar…

Forecasts use each lab’s full release history and ignore filters. These are projections from past rhythm, not predictions of fact. Open a lab to see the gaps they’re built from.

How the forecast is calculated
  1. Releases collapse to distinct launch days, so a same-day Pro + Flash launch is one event, not a 0-day gap.
  2. Gaps are the days between consecutive launch days. The window is the middle half of those gaps (Q1–Q3), the centre is the median, and both are counted from the lab’s last release. With fewer than 4 gaps the window widens to ±30% of the median; with fewer than 2, the pooled gap distribution of all labs is used.
  3. Status: overdue (today is past the window), in window, or window opens in N days. Beyond 2.5× the median a lab reads as “unusually quiet” instead; it may have changed cadence rather than be late.
  4. Likely weekday is the most common weekday of launch days. Likely hour is the 3-hour window holding the most timed releases (UTC); it is shown only with ≥ 3 known times and ≥ 2 in the window.
  5. Evidence: 8+ gaps is solid, 4–7 fair, fewer is thin. Frequent shippers get narrow windows, so many labs can read as due at once.
